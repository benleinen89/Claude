## Posting notes

- Post it from a person's profile (a founder or someone on the team), then reshare from the Poksundo company page. Personal profiles usually reach more people.
- Reply to every comment in the first 60 to 90 minutes. Comments in that window carry the most weight.
- Tue to Thu, 8 to 10 am in the audience's main time zone.
- Burn in captions. Most people watch LinkedIn video with the sound off.
